Rendered in (see inbox_nav.html) and moved into the page once it loads. - Links and buttons marked data-nbca-modal load their confirmation page's dialog version into it. Without JavaScript they open the full page. - Buttons marked data-nbca-confirm="Question?" ask first, in place of the browser's OK/Cancel box. Optional: data-nbca-confirm-label, data-nbca-confirm-class. {% endcomment %}
